# Model credits

dsi.glb is a reduced and re-rigged copy of a third-party model, built by
`scripts/build-dsi-model.mjs`. The credit line below is the one the author's
download asked for, copied verbatim:

This work is based on "DSi" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/dsi-2d03bc1178a54658872030e22ac77c35) by Cleanman00 (https://sketchfab.com/Cleanman00) licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

Changes made: transforms baked in, the lid separated from the base so it can
hinge, all 264k triangles kept (bevels and button rims intact), textures resized to 2048 px for the shell and
buttons and 1024 px for the rest and re-encoded as WebP, the plastics made
non-metallic and the buttons a little glossier than the shell, the shell's
mottled wear texture flattened to an even near-black (printed marks kept) and
its scanned normal and roughness maps removed, original animation removed.
